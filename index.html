Amazon Delivery Performance Analysis

This project analyzes logistics delivery performance data to identify key factors affecting delivery speed, efficiency, and agent ratings. By combining Python and ML, the goal is to uncover operational bottlenecks and provide data-driven recommendations to improve delivery reliability and reduce delays.

Business Problem


Logistics companies often face challenges such as late deliveries, inconsistent carrier performance, and regional inefficiencies. This project hopes to answer:
  • What factors contribute the most to delivery delays (distance, areas, weather)?
  • Which areas or categories perform best or worst?
  • How do delivery delays impact Agent Ratings?
  • Can we predict whether a delivery will be slow?

    • Python · Pandas & NumPy · Matplotlib & Seaborn · Scikit-learn · Google Colab · Streamlit
